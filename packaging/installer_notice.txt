WHAT SETUP DOES
- Installs V-COMMS for your Windows account only (no administrator rights needed).
- Installs VB-CABLE, the free virtual audio cable V-COMMS needs, only if this PC does not have it yet.
  Installing that driver is the one step Windows asks permission for.
- Keeps your settings and presets in your AppData folder, so upgrades never lose them.

V-COMMS never reads or writes game memory and never installs keyboard hooks.
